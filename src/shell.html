Recipe Book

Add a recipe yourself with the two steps below, or ask in chat — e.g. add taro bun from rasa malaysia.

Step 1 — Build the prompt

Paste a recipe link, or just name the dish and the site. Then copy the prompt into ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini — whichever you like.

Step 2 — Paste the result

Paste the JSON the AI gave you and import. It's checked before anything is added, and saved in this browser.

Portable copy

Paste this into another app or hand it to Claude to build against. It's a snapshot — re-export after you add recipes.

Your data

Favourites, your notes, servings, crossed-off steps, removed recipes and anything you've imported live only in this browser. Clear its site data and they're gone. This is the only copy — keep it somewhere safe.

Restore

Paste a backup to merge it in. Nothing is deleted — favourites and removals are added to what's already here, and notes for the same recipe are overwritten by the backup's version.
